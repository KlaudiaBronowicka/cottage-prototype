## A: THE DOMOWIK
WHAT IT IS: The guesthouse's domowik: a small, old house spirit who lives behind the stove and walks the edge of the holding at night to keep out the voice in the trees. / The house is being sold and nobody has fed it in months; it comes to the door frightened and sulking, wanting to be noticed, and its alarm is what draws COMPANION 1 outside.
AT THE DOOR (pick one):
- Three knocks, low on the door, like someone very short. Then the stove goes out.
- Something small rattles the handle, impatient, and every pan in the kitchen rings at once.
- A sigh at the keyhole, old and tired. The kitchen goes cold.
COMPANION 1: It sounds lonely. Someone should go out to it.
TURN AWAY:
- Comfort: POV: [the way the HOST talks to the stove] Dziadku, we're good guests. Go back to your warm corner.
- Offering: The heel of the bread, salted, and the last of supper, left by the stove. The pacing stops.
- Bar: The chair holds. On the other side, something small checks the door twice, and seems satisfied.
EVENT TEASES:
- Footsteps: Small steps, circling. Counting the windows.
- The Gate Creaks: Someone left the gate open. Someone minds.
- Silence: The stove stops ticking. The house is listening.
- The Door Handle: Something short is reaching for the handle.
NIGHTS 5-6: The sky over the trees goes grey, and the pacing gets faster, as if it knows.
TREELINE SEARCH (POV, inner): Every step I take into the trees, I hear it hurry back toward the house, like I've left a door open.

## B: THE ZMORA
WHAT IT IS: The zmora: a night spirit that settles on sleepers' chests, in many Polish accounts the wandering soul of a living person who doesn't know they walk. / It circles the cottage looking for a way in to whoever's guilt is heaviest; it isn't hateful, it's hungry and lost, and it has to be home before dawn.
AT THE DOOR (pick one):
- The handle doesn't turn. Something comes through the keyhole anyway, thin as a straw.
- A weight settles on the end of the bed. Nobody is sitting there.
- Everyone's breath goes short at once, as if the air got heavy.
COMPANION 1: [sitting up, eyes open, not awake] I can't breathe in here. I need air.
TURN AWAY:
- Comfort: COMPANION 2: [softly, by her ear] COMPANION 1. COMPANION 1, wake up. It's us.
- Offering: POV: [whispering into the dark] Come in the morning. I'll give you bread and salt. [The weight lifts.]
- Bar: The chair holds, and the key stays half-turned in the lock. Nothing comes through the keyhole.
EVENT TEASES:
- Footsteps: Bare feet in the wet grass. Nobody's shoes are missing.
- The Gate Creaks: The gate swings. Whoever it is doesn't need it.
- Silence: Even the breathing in the next room stops.
- The Door Handle: The handle won't turn. It doesn't have to.
NIGHTS 5-6: The sky thins. Whatever is out there is in a hurry now: it has to be back in its own body before light.
TREELINE SEARCH (POV, inner): Out here I can breathe. Back there someone can't, and it knows I'm not watching.
